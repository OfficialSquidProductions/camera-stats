Release notes & checksums · Mac & mobile reporting alpha

Windows desktop 0.2.0-alpha.1 · local media review

Windows adds frame review, manual slate confirmation, report association, saved naming presets, and verified copies with resumable journals and non-destructive undo. Originals stay unchanged.

The Windows x64 download is an unsigned manual MSI installer. The signed, notarized universal Mac reporting alpha remains on 0.1.1; its new media-review build is undergoing validation.

Mobile stays on 0.1.1: the signed Android APK is unchanged; iPhone, iPad and Mac TestFlight 0.1.1 (2) remain available to the invited account owner.

The record behind the shot.

  • Log scene and take labels, camera rolls, lens and exposure settings, and MOS/pickup/tail-slate flags.
  • Keep camera remarks, DP selections, continuity notes, and editorial notes separately.
  • Use Quick, Standard, and Full take views without losing recorded information.
  • Complete a student camera report with a missing-field checklist and self-review.
  • Preview a landscape PDF, export CSV/HTML, and transfer editable .camreport files deliberately.
  • Windows desktop: review MOV/MP4 frames, confirm scene/take labels, and associate footage with reviewed report entries.
  • Windows desktop: preview named copies, verify bytes and metadata, resume interrupted work, and retain undo recovery files.
  • Open The Last Light demo: the eight-scene script and 16-shot plan shared with Production Desk and Scenic, plus three completed 3A takes with take 2 circled.

Projects stay on your device. No account, subscription, cloud sync, or telemetry. Keep an independent local backup.

Platform status

  • iPhone / iPad: native field alpha · iOS / iPadOS 17+ · owner TestFlight available.
  • Mac: reporting alpha 0.1.1 · macOS 14+ · universal Intel / Apple silicon · signed, notarized download. New media-review build undergoing validation.
  • Android phones / tablets: field alpha · Android 8+ · signed APK download · tested on Android 15 phone/tablet emulators.
  • Windows: desktop alpha · Windows 10+ x64 · bundled runtime and media tools · unsigned manual MSI download.

Physical device and camera testing is still needed. Desktop validation uses isolated report storage; keep independent backups of reports and footage. Automatic update delivery remains subject to the platform signing and native upgrade gates described in the release notes.

Experimental RAW adapters require separately installed, authorized REDline or a locally built Blackmagic RAW helper and SDK runtime. No vendor SDK, decoder, or sample footage is included. Local Mac tests covered a two-span RED sample and short BRAW fixtures; Windows native RAW, long-clip/multicard BRAW, slate OCR and NLE relink certification remain outstanding. See the RAW setup and validation limits.
