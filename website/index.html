Version 0.1 · owner-test field alpha

Windows and Android field alpha downloads are available above. The Android APK is signed; Windows installers are unsigned. The universal Mac alpha is signed and notarized by Apple. Shot Reporter 0.1.0 (1) remains available to the account owner in TestFlight for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Open TestFlight with the invited Apple Account.

This is an early reporting milestone. The complete footage and RAW workflow is still in development.

The record behind the shot.

  • Log scene and take labels, camera rolls, lens and exposure settings, and MOS/pickup/tail-slate flags.
  • Keep camera remarks, DP selections, continuity notes, and editorial notes separately.
  • Use Quick, Standard, and Full take views without losing recorded information.
  • Complete a student camera report with a missing-field checklist and self-review.
  • Preview a landscape PDF, export CSV/HTML, and transfer editable .camreport files deliberately.
  • Open The Last Light demo: the eight-scene script and 16-shot plan shared with Production Desk and Scenic, plus three completed 3A takes with take 2 circled.

Projects stay on your device. No account, subscription, cloud sync, or telemetry. Keep an independent local backup.

Platform status

  • iPhone / iPad: native field alpha · iOS / iPadOS 17+ · owner TestFlight available.
  • Mac: native field alpha · macOS 14+ · universal Intel / Apple silicon · signed, notarized download available.
  • Android phones / tablets: field alpha · Android 8+ · signed APK download · tested on Android 15 phone/tablet emulators.
  • Windows: field alpha · Windows 10+ x64 · bundled runtime · unsigned MSI / EXE downloads.

Physical Android and Windows device testing is still needed. The new clients passed native storage, report export/import, demo, layout and installer checks in isolated test machines.

Slate OCR, exact-frame footage browsing, physical RED R3D / Blackmagic RAW renaming, and NLE adapters require more implementation and real-media validation. Naming previews never change camera footage. No camera or NLE certification is claimed.
